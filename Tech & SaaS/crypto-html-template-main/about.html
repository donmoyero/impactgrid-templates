The exchange built on trust & time

Meridian started in 2021 with a simple thesis: digital-asset markets deserve exchange infrastructure that is boringly safe, brutally fast and built to outlast hype cycles.

Our Story

From two engineers to a $8B market

Meridian began in a Berlin kitchen in 2021, when two trading-infrastructure engineers grew tired of exchanges that treated uptime as a suggestion. They wanted custody so cold it made accountants shiver, and a matching engine that never flinched.

Today Meridian serves 4.2 million traders across 140 countries, clears over $8 billion in daily volume and holds the industry's most transparent reserve attestations. The kitchen table became a boardroom, but the obsession never left.

  • Regulated entities in three jurisdictions
  • Quarterly proof-of-reserve audits since 2022
  • Zero security breaches in operating history
Our Values

What we refuse to compromise

Four principles govern every decision we make — from how we hold funds to how we write a single line of code.

Security First

Cold storage, multi-signature quorums and quarterly attestations. Trust is earned one audited quarter at a time.

Radical Transparency

Reserves on-chain, fees public, status page honest. If we can't explain it plainly, we don't ship it.

Long-Term Thinking

We measure success in decades of uptime, not a quarter of trading volume. Boring is a feature.

Customer Obsession

A human support desk, a plain-language status page and fees that reward our most loyal traders.
