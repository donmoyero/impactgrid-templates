FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Everything founders usually ask before starting. Still curious? Reach out — we answer within one business day.

What exactly does IGNITE do? +

IGNITE is a product-launch platform for startups. It combines launch playbooks, collaboration tools, beta validation, and analytics into one workflow — so a team can go from idea to a coordinated public launch without stitching together a dozen disconnected tools.

How does the free plan work? +

The Starter plan is free forever — no credit card required. It includes one active launch project, core playbooks, and support from our community. Upgrade to Growth whenever you need unlimited projects, advanced analytics, or beta cohort tools.

Can I change or cancel my plan at any time? +

Yes. You can upgrade, downgrade, or cancel in two clicks from your billing settings. Changes take effect at the start of your next billing cycle, and you keep full access to your data — you can export everything at any time.

Do you provide hands-on launch support? +

Growth and Enterprise plans include priority support from our team, and Enterprise adds a dedicated launch advisor plus a launch-day command centre. We're in the trenches with you when it matters most.

How long does a typical launch take? +

It depends on your starting point. Teams using our playbooks typically compress their go-to-market from six months to six weeks. A simple MVP validation sprint can take as little as two weeks from kickoff to first users.

What if I'm pre-product — do you help with validation? +

Absolutely. That's often the best moment to start. Use our beta cohort tools to recruit early users, test a landing page and waitlist, and collect structured feedback — before you invest in full development.
