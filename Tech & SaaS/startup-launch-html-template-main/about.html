The story behind IGNITE

We exist for one reason: too many great products never reach the market they deserve.

Our story

Born from a thousand launches gone sideways

IGNITE started in 2019 when our founders — fresh off two exits — kept meeting startup after startup with a familiar problem: a brilliant product sitting in a launch limbo of indecision, scattered tools, and missed momentum.

  • ✓
    Founded by operators

    Every founder at IGNITE has personally launched products to millions of users. We've felt the launch-day nerves, and we've learned what makes them unnecessary.

  • ✓
    A playbook, not a promise

    What started as an internal launch methodology grew into a platform — codifying hundreds of launches into repeatable, data-backed playbooks.

  • ✓
    Built for the builders

    Today, 2,000+ startups across 120 countries use IGNITE to launch with confidence, from bootstrapped two-person teams to venture-backed scale-ups.
