About STUDIO77

Our Story

Since 2018

A Vision Born from Passion

STUDIO77 was founded on a simple belief: that a hair salon should be more than a place for a haircut. It should be a sanctuary where artistry, luxury, and personal expression converge.

What began as a small studio in Manhattan's vibrant SoHo district has grown into one of New York's most sought-after hair destinations. Our founder, with over 15 years of experience at the world's leading salons, envisioned a space where every client receives the time, attention, and expertise they deserve.

Today, STUDIO77 stands as a testament to that vision, welcoming clients from around the world who seek not just a style, but an experience.

Our Philosophy

The Art of Hair

We believe that exceptional hair care is a craft that demands both technical mastery and creative intuition.

Master Craft, Personal Touch

At STUDIO77, we don't believe in one-size-fits-all. Every face shape, every hair texture, every lifestyle tells a different story. Our role is to listen, understand, and then create something uniquely yours.

We invest in continuous education, sending our stylists to train with masters in Paris, Milan, and Tokyo. This global perspective, combined with a deep understanding of individual client needs, is what sets us apart.

Every product we use, every technique we employ, every consultation we hold is guided by one principle: your hair should be the most authentic expression of who you are.
