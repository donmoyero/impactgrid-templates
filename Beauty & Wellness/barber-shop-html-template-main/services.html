What We Do in the Chair

From the barber's chair to the mirror — every service, explained.

01 Classic Haircut

A consultation-first haircut that respects your bone structure, hair texture and daily routine. Clipper, scissor and straight-razor work for a clean, lasting finish. Every cut ends with a hot-towel neck tidy and a tonic mist.

  • Scissor-over-comb texture
  • Clipper fade & taper
  • Razor-neck line up
  • Hot-towel finish
$38 45 min Book Now

02 Hot Towel Shave

The full straight-razor ritual — hot towels to open the pores, pre-shave oil, a lather built with a badger brush, and a blade honed to surgical sharpness. Cold towel close, aftershave balm, and a handshake. This is the closest shave you will ever get.

  • Hot towel prep
  • Badger-brush lather
  • Straight-razor passes
  • Cold towel & balm
$42 50 min Book Now

03 Beard Sculpting

Line work, shape and conditioning for the modern beard. We use scissor, clipper and straight-razor detailing to define cheek lines, necklines and the mustache border. Finished with beard oil and a boar-bristle brush.

  • Cheek & neck line-up
  • Scissor shape & bulk
  • Razor-edge detailing
  • Oil & brush finish
$30 25 min Book Now
