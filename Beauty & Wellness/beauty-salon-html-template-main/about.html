Welcome to a Space That Nurtures

LUMIÈRE was founded by Ava Laurent with a simple belief: beauty should feel like care, not rush. Our brownstone studio in Brooklyn is designed to be a quiet retreat from the city — a place where you're known by name and your preferences are remembered.

We use cruelty-free, professional-grade products and invest continuously in our team's training so every treatment delivers the best results. From a quick trim to a full bridal trial, you'll leave feeling lighter, brighter, and truly seen.
