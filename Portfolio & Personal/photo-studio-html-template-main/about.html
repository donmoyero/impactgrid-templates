The studio behind the shutter

Twelve years, one darkroom, and a stubborn belief that a photograph should be kept, not merely posted. This is where that belief comes from.

Since 2013

It began with one rented darkroom and a roll of film

Every studio has an origin story. Ours starts with the smell of fixer and a single borrowed enlarger.

Photozone was founded by a photographer who refused to believe that haste makes better pictures. That first year we shot weddings, ID portraits, product shots and a hundred other things just to keep the lights on — and learned something from every single frame.

Today the studio is a full team: photographers, retouchers and producers who share the same slow, deliberate approach. The darkroom is gone, but the discipline it taught us — look twice, shoot once, keep only what matters — still runs through every job we take.

What We Stand On

Four habits that shape every frame

We are not a big studio trying to act small. We are a deliberately small studio that refuses to grow past the point where we can still care about every print.

Natural light first

We chase the light before we reach for the strobes. When artificial light is needed, it is shaped to feel unforced.

Less, but better

We would rather deliver forty frames you love than four hundred you scroll past. Editing is part of the craft.

Hands-on finishing

Every selection is graded by the photographer who shot it — no outsourcing, no mystery colour shifts.

Kept, not archived

Fine-art prints, archival storage and printed albums. We make photographs you can hand down.
