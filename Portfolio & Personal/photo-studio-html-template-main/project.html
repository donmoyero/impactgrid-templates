Behind the Frames

A closer look at two projects

The thinking behind the image — concept, light, process and the story the client wanted to tell.

Wedding · 2024

Golden Hour

A late-summer wedding at a countryside estate. The brief was to capture the weight of the day without staging a single moment. We shot with natural light only, using the architecture as a frame within the frame.

The result is a documentary-style gallery that the couple described as "how we remember it, not how we posed for it."

Documentary · 2024

In the Open

A portrait series for a conservation non-profit working across remote landscapes. The brief asked for images that conveyed scale, solitude and the quiet dignity of the people who live there.

We travelled light — two bodies, three primes, available light only. The series was later exhibited at a gallery in London.
