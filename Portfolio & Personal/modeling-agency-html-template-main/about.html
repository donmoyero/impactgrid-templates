The studio

STORY

Manifesto

The frame is everything.

We founded Poseify in 2019 with a simple belief — that the right face, at the right moment, can change how a collection is seen. We're not a volume agency. We're a casting studio that happens to represent talent.

Every model on our board is personally scouted, mentored and moved by a booker who knows their name, their walk and the shot they're made for. We don't build books. We build careers. Our approach is rooted in the editorial traditions of Milan, the energy of New York and the precision of Paris — three cities that shape everything we do.

We work with casting directors, creative studios and houses who refuse to settle for "good enough." Because good enough still walks the runway, but it doesn't own it.

Milan Studio

What we stand for

Craft · Integrity · Inclusion

01

Craft

We train our models like athletes — movement, presence, editorial instinct. Every face on our board is developed for the specific demands of runway, print and motion.

02

Integrity

Transparent contracts, fair fees, zero exploitation. We believe a model's career should be built on trust, not fine print. Our reps are hands-on, always.

03

Inclusion

Beauty is not a single note. We represent faces across gender, ethnicity, size, age and identity — because the future of fashion is expansive, not exclusive.

Our timeline

From Milan to the world.

Poseify founded 2019 — Milan

Launched in a small Via della Spiga studio with five models, one booker and a conviction that the industry deserved a different kind of agency.

Milan office 2021 — Milan

Expanded to a full atelier space in the Brera district. Doubled the roster and signed our first exclusive beauty campaign.

Paris desk 2023 — Paris

Opened a Paris office to cover fashion week, editorial bookings and the growing demand from European luxury houses.

New York + 120 models 2025 — New York

Crossed 120 represented models, opened a New York desk and expanded into commercial casting, e-commerce and film.
