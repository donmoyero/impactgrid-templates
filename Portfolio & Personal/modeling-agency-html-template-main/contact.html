FAQ

Questions answered

How do I book a model for a campaign?
Send us a brief via the form above or email hello@poseify.agency with your requirements — we'll respond with curated boards, pricing and availability within 24 hours.
Can I submit my portfolio to Poseify?
Yes. We accept digital submissions via the form above. Include your name, location, measurements, digitals and a link to your portfolio. We review every submission within two weeks.
What cities do you cover for casting?
Our primary desks are in Milan, Paris and New York. We also have network partners in London, Berlin, Lagos, Nairobi and Tokyo for international casting.
What are your rates for commercial vs editorial?
Rates vary by usage, territory and exclusivity. We provide transparent pricing per brief and never add hidden fees. Contact us for a tailored quote.
