What we cast

Four disciplines, one standard — every brief answered with a face, a fit and a story that lands.

SERVICES
01

Runway & Show Casting

End-to-end casting for fashion weeks, brand presentations and designer showcases. From the first brief to final fittings, we curate boards that walk with intent.

  • Fashion week casting (Milan, Paris, NY)
  • Brand presentations & showrooms
  • Fittings, measurements & tech packs
  • Backstage coordination & travel
02

Editorial & Campaign

Models matched to art direction by our casting directors — for magazine stories, lookbooks and full-scale campaigns that run across print and digital.

  • Magazine editorials & covers
  • Lookbooks & seasonal collections
  • Brand campaigns & key visuals
  • Beauty & close-up specialists
03

Commercial & Digital

Faces built for conversion as much as for the cover — e-commerce, film, motion and social-first talent that performs in front of the lens and the camera.

  • E-commerce & catalogue casting
  • Commercial film & motion
  • Social & UGC talent
  • Fast-turnaround digital briefs
04

Talent Management

Long-term representation for serious careers — development, bookings, contracts and global movement under one roof, with a booker who answers their phone.

  • Career strategy & development
  • Bookings & contracts
  • Global movement & visas
  • Portfolio & test shoots

How we work

Four beats to showtime.

Consult

Tell us the brief, the budget and the vision. We advise on the right casting strategy and timeline.

Cast

Our bookers shortlist faces and present boards with measurements, digitals and campaign history.

Shoot

We coordinate fittings, travel and on-set support so the only thing your team handles is the creative.

Deliver

Post-production hand-off, usage licensing and billing handled in one clean final invoice.
