Our Story

Built on Trust, Powered by Expertise

Founded in 1998, Electra began as a small family-run electrical shop in Austin, Texas. What started with a single truck and a commitment to honest work has grown into one of the region's most trusted electrical contracting firms, serving residential, commercial, and industrial clients across the state.

Our founder, David Hartwell, built the company on a simple principle: every electrical system we touch must be safer, more reliable, and more efficient than when we arrived. That philosophy drives every project we undertake today, from a simple outlet repair to a multi-phase industrial installation.

Over 25 years later, we maintain that same dedication to craftsmanship and customer care. Our team of 100+ licensed electricians brings decades of combined experience to every job, ensuring code-compliant work that stands the test of time.
