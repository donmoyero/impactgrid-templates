Our Story

About GARAGE

Fifteen years of turning wrenches, solving puzzles, and earning trust. We started with a two-bay shop and a simple promise: honest work at fair prices.

Since 2009

From Two Bays
to a Full-Service Shop

What began as a small family garage has grown into a 12-bay, full-service repair center. But our core belief hasn't changed: every customer deserves transparency, quality, and respect.

We invested in the latest diagnostic equipment, trained our team on emerging EV and hybrid systems, and built a waiting room where you'd actually want to spend time. Because auto repair shouldn't be stressful.
