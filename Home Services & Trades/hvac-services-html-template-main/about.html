Our Story

Built on Trust

CLIMATECONTROL was founded in 2005 by Mike Torres, a master HVAC technician with a vision: provide honest, reliable HVAC service at fair prices.

Starting with a single truck and a toolbox, we've grown into a team of 30+ certified technicians serving residential and commercial clients across the region.

Our mission remains the same: treat every customer's home like our own, and never recommend a repair that isn't necessary.
