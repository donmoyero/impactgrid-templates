About IronWatch

Twenty-five years of disciplined guarding. A single standard: never compromise.

Our Story

Built on the belief that real security is personal

IronWatch was founded by former law-enforcement and military professionals who saw too many security companies put uniforms before accountability. We exist to prove that professional guarding can be both visible and reliable — every shift, every post.

Today we operate across three states, deploying over 480 officers to more than 2,300 active sites. Our operations desk runs 24/7, directing patrols, managing alarms and ensuring every guard has the support they need to do their job right.

We are licensed, bonded and fully insured. Our training curriculum meets or exceeds every state standard — and we add our own modules on de-escalation, report writing, first aid and site-specific risk awareness.
