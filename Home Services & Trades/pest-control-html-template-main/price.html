Got Questions?

Pricing FAQ

Do you really include a free inspection?
Yes. Every new plan or one-time service starts with a no-obligation inspection. We walk the property, identify the pest pressure, and give you a written quote before any work begins. If you decide not to proceed, there's zero charge.
Are there any hidden fees or upcharges?
None. The quote we give you is the price you pay — no surprise trip charges, no "per-pest" add-ons, no seasonal rate hikes. If the scope changes mid-project, we communicate that before we proceed.
What is the 30-day re-service guarantee?
If pests return within 30 days of a treatment visit, we come back at no charge. This covers the same pest species and same treatment areas — we don't believe in charging twice for the same problem.
Can I switch plans mid-contract?
Absolutely. Upgrade or downgrade anytime with no penalty. Your billing adjusts from the next billing cycle. We'd rather you get the coverage you need than stick to a plan that doesn't fit.
Do you offer commercial pricing?
Yes. Commercial and multi-family programs are quoted per property based on square footage, pest pressure and compliance requirements. Contact our commercial team for a tailored proposal.
What payment methods do you accept?
Credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, Amex), e-checks and ACH transfers. Monthly plans are auto-billed on the same date each cycle. One-time services are due upon completion.
