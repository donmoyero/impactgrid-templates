Featured Case Study

Lakeshore Estate
Termite Barrier

A 1924 craftsman on the lakeshore had active subterranean termites in the crawlspace and mud tubes climbing the foundation sill. The owner had already lost a section of floor joist.

We installed a complete liquid soil barrier around the foundation, treated active galleries, and set monitoring stations in high-risk zones. Full remediation took five days with zero disruption to the family's routine. The property has been termite-free for 18 months and counting.

  • Scope — liquid barrier, gallery treatment, monitoring stations
  • Result — 0 signs of termite activity in 18 months
  • Warranty — transferable termite warranty included
