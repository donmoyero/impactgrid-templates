Our Story

From one truck to a 28-person brigade

In 2011 our founder, a former state inspector, saw the same pattern in the field every week — over-spraying, under-reporting, and customers left guessing what was actually done.

PestKit was built around a different contract: inspect before you treat, quote before you touch, and document everything. Today our 28 certified specialists run the same discipline across 15,000+ properties — from century-old craftsman homes to 40-floor commercial towers.

  • Fully licensed & insured in every state we operate.
  • Integrated Pest Management — prevention-first, chemical-last.
  • Continuous training — every tech recertifies every year.
