Our story

Founded on a
simple feeling

In 2008, we watched too many HVAC companies sell fear — upselling repairs, quoting without diagnostics, and rushing out the door.

We built ZEPHYR on the opposite instinct: slow down, measure, and explain. Every visit starts with a full system readout. Every quote comes with a written report. Every technician carries thermal cameras and digital gauges — not just wrenches.

Today we care for more than 12,000 systems across the metro — homes, offices, and storefronts — and our maintenance-plan clients average 18% lower energy bills and 73% fewer emergency calls.
