Our Story

Built by technicians, run like a command centre

Vigilant started in the back of a van with a crimping kit and a conviction that most "security" systems were theatre. We install the real thing: cameras placed for evidence-grade angles, cabling that outlasts the building, and monitoring that treats every alert as a question to answer — not a notification to ignore.

Today our command centre watches over retail floors, warehouse yards, office towers and family homes across three states. The van is bigger now. The conviction hasn't changed.

  • Independent and accountable

    We design, supply, install and monitor — so there is exactly one number to call when anything needs attention.
  • Evidence-grade by default

    Every install targets prosecution-ready image quality and time-stamped, tamper-evident recordings — not just "something visible".

Operating Principles

The standards we don't negotiate

PRINCIPLE_01

Coverage before cosmetics

We never place a camera where it looks good instead of where it sees. Every lens position earns its keep against a documented coverage map.

PRINCIPLE_02

Trust but verify

Our operators verify every alarm with live footage before dispatch. It cuts false alarms dramatically and makes real ones count for more.

PRINCIPLE_03

Your footage stays yours

Encryption, strict access logging and local-first storage. We hold the keys to your keys, not to your privacy.

PRINCIPLE_04

Fixed, honest pricing

What we quote is what you pay. No call-out surprises, no "while we're here" upselling on site.

PRINCIPLE_05

Uptime is uptime

Proactive health checks, redundant recorders and a 99.7% monitoring availability target. A dead camera is a failure we own.

PRINCIPLE_06

Train the customer too

Every handover includes a full walkthrough — how to pull footage, check storage, and use the app like an operator would.
