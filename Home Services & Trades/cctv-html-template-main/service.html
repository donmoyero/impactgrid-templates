FAQ

Questions we answer every week

How long does an installation take?

Most homes complete in one day; typical retail and office sites take 2–5 days; large industrial multi-site rollouts run 1–3 weeks. We give you a precise timeline in your fixed-price proposal.

Can I watch my cameras on my phone?

Yes. Every system ships with encrypted apps for iOS and Android plus a desktop dashboard. Live feeds typically connect in under three seconds from anywhere.

Do you store my footage in the cloud?

We default to local-first recording on your own NVR — you own the hardware and the footage. Encrypted cloud backup is available as an optional add-on from 7 to 365 days of retention.

What happens if the power or internet goes out?

Cameras and recorders run on battery-backed power, and monitoring stations use dual uplinks. Recording continues locally through outages, then syncs when the connection returns.

Are your installers certified and insured?

Every technician is manufacturer-certified, background-checked and covered by our full liability insurance. Your contract names the crew — no anonymous subcontractors.
