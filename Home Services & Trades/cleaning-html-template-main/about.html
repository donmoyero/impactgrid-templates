Our Story

From One Van to the Region's Most Trusted Cleaners

PureLine began in 2014 with a single van, a box of microfibre cloths and a stubborn belief that cleaning could be done differently — with systems, accountability and genuine care.

Today we run a 120-strong team covering hundreds of homes and businesses every week. But the formula hasn't changed: trained people, documented checklists, eco-friendly products and a re-clean guarantee on every single job.

  • ✓ A 22-point inspection on every residential clean
  • ✓ Dedicated account managers for commercial clients
  • ✓ Ongoing staff training and industry certification
