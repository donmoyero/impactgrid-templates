What We Do

A Service for Every Space and Standard

Every service includes our certified team, eco-friendly products and the PureLine re-clean guarantee.

Home Cleaning

Dusting, vacuuming, mopping, kitchens and bathrooms — on a schedule that suits your life, from weekly to one-off.

Office & Commercial

Flexible contracts for offices, retail and clinics — sanitised touchpoints, gleaming floors and tidy common areas.

Carpet & Upholstery

Hot-water extraction lifts deep-set dirt, allergens and odours from carpets, rugs, sofas and mattresses.

Window Cleaning

Streak-free glass inside and out, with frames, tracks and sills included. Reach heights handled safely and insured.

Deep & Spring Cleaning

A top-to-bottom seasonal overhaul: behind appliances, inside cabinets, baseboards, grout and every forgotten corner.

Move-In / Move-Out

End-of-lease and pre-move cleans designed to pass the strictest inspections and protect your deposit.
