Our Story

Two decades of paint, prep and pride

TrueColor began as a two-person crew with one van and a stubborn idea: that a paint job should feel like craft, not coverage.

Today we’re a studio of master painters, colour consultants and project leads — but the obsession hasn’t changed. We still measure twice, mask everything and walk every wall with you before we call it done.

  • Founded in Maple District, still family-owned today
  • Master painter on every job, never subcontracted blind
  • Colour-matched to any swatch, sample or photo
  • 10-year workmanship guarantee, in writing
