Our Story

From a Two-Man Shop to Portland’s Most Trusted

PipeCraft started in 1998 with a single van, a well-worn pipe wrench, and a promise: show up on time, quote a fair price, and do the job right the first time. Twenty-five years later that promise still runs through everything we do.

Today we employ a full crew of licensed plumbers, HVAC technicians, and comfort specialists serving the whole Portland metro. We’ve traded the single van for a fleet, but the flat-rate honesty and craftsmanship that built our name haven’t changed a bit.

  • Licensed, bonded, and insured in Oregon
  • Family-owned and locally operated
  • Fully background-checked technicians
  • Green-practice water-saving recommendations
