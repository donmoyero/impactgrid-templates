About IRONARC

A quarter-century of welding excellence, built one clean bead at a time.

Our Story

Forged in Fire

IRONARC started in 1999 as a two-man shop in a rented warehouse. The founder, a former underwater welder, believed that a properly executed weld should outlast the structure it joins — and that belief became the company's standard.

Today we operate a 40,000-square-foot facility with 85+ certified welders, in-house engineering, CNC cutting, and a full metallurgical lab. We serve contractors, plant managers, fabricators, and architects across the region, and every project — from a single stair railing to a multi-story steel frame — carries the same 3-year warranty.

Our Standards

Certifications & Values

We weld to published codes, not opinions. Every certification is earned, every procedure is documented, and every team member is accountable.

🏅

AWS D1.1 Certified

Our structural welding procedures are qualified and maintained under the American Welding Society D1.1 structural code — the gold standard for steel construction.

📋

ISO 9001:2015

A fully documented quality management system covering every step from material receiving to final inspection, with annual external audits and continuous improvement tracking.

🛡️

OSHA Compliant & Safety-First

Zero-compromise safety culture with daily toolbox talks, a full-time safety officer, and a 5-year incident-free record. Every jobsite operates under a site-specific safety plan.
