Meet The Makers

Six people, one standard

No apprentices dropped in for a season, no subcontractors on a busy month. The team you meet is the team that builds.

Founder & Master Carpenter

Marcus Bell

Thirty years at the bench, eighteen at Craft & Grain. Marcus still draws every kitchen by hand.

Measure twice.
Head Designer

Elena Sorensen

A furniture designer who sketches in the workshop, not just on screen — so nothing gets lost in translation.

Draw it like you will build it.
Cabinetmaker

Jamal Pierce

The dovetail specialist. If a joint is going to be visible, Jamal is the one who wants it perfect.

Joinery is honesty.
Finisher
