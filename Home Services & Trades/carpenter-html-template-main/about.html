Our Story

From one bench to a family workshop

Craft & Grain began in 2008 with a single workbench, a second-hand jointer and a stubborn belief that homes deserve better than flat-pack. Eighteen years later, it's a six-person workshop — and we still build the same way.

  • Named for what matters

    The craft is the joinery; the grain is the timber. Both are honest, both are in every piece.

  • Six craftsmen, one roof

    Designers, cabinetmakers and finishers who have worked together for a decade.

  • We answer the phone

    No call centres, no subcontractors. When you call, you get the person building your piece.

What We Stand For

Four rules we never bend

The short list pinned above the bench — the values that keep every piece honest.

01

Material Honesty

If it says oak, it is oak. No veneers over softwood, no shortcuts behind a painted face.

02

Joinery First

Structures are joined, not screwed and hidden. If a joint is visible, it should be worth seeing.

03

Fixed Prices

A quote is a promise. We price the work honestly once, and we don't find surprises on site.

04

Built to Last

A 25-year guarantee isn't a marketing line. It's the minimum we expect a good piece to survive.
