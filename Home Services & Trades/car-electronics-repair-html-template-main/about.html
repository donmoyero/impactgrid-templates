About CEPAIR

15+ years of certified car electronics expertise — diagnostics, audio, infotainment, and smart upgrades for every vehicle.

// Our Story

Precision Diagnostics Since 2009

CEPAIR was founded with a simple mission: bring factory-level electronic diagnostics and repair to independent car owners. What started as a one-bay workshop has grown into Austin's most trusted car electronics destination.

Our team of ASE-certified technicians specializes in everything from parasitic drain diagnosis to full infotainment overhauls. We invest in the same diagnostic equipment used by dealership service departments — but deliver it with the personal attention of a specialist shop.
