Age-Appropriate Learning Paths

Each program is carefully designed to meet the developmental needs of its age group while making learning fun and engaging.

Ages 2-3

Tiny Explorers

Our Tiny Explorers program introduces our youngest learners to the wonders of discovery through sensory-rich activities, gentle routines, and nurturing care. Children develop fine motor skills, early language, and social confidence in a safe, loving environment.

🕐 Schedule: Mon-Fri, 9:00 AM - 12:00 PM
👶 Class Size: Max 10 children
👩‍🏫 Staff Ratio: 1:5
🎨 Activities: Sensory play, music, storytime

Ages 3-4

Creative Kids

Creative Kids combines art, music, and storytelling to build essential early literacy and numeracy skills. Through hands-on projects and collaborative play, children develop their imagination, vocabulary, and social skills while having a blast.

🕐 Schedule: Mon-Fri, 9:00 AM - 2:00 PM
👶 Class Size: Max 15 children
👩‍🏫 Staff Ratio: 1:7
🎨 Activities: Art, music, dramatic play, literacy

Ages 4-5

Bright Minds

Our pre-kindergarten program prepares children for school success through a balanced curriculum of early reading, math concepts, science exploration, and social-emotional learning. Children build confidence and independence while mastering foundational skills.

🕐 Schedule: Mon-Fri, 8:30 AM - 3:00 PM
👶 Class Size: Max 18 children
👩‍🏫 Staff Ratio: 1:9
🎨 Activities: Reading, math, science, field trips
