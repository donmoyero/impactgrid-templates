Good to Know

Frequently asked questions

Do you offer gluten-free or vegan options?

Yes! We bake a rotating selection of gluten-free and vegan breads and pastries each morning, clearly labelled on the shelf. Custom dietary orders for cakes are available with advance notice.

How far in advance should I order a cake?

For celebration cakes we recommend 72 hours’ notice. Wedding towers and large events should be booked 2–4 weeks ahead to secure your preferred date.

Do you deliver wholesale orders?

Absolutely. We deliver fresh bread and pastries to cafés, hotels, and offices within the city every morning. Contact us for a tailored wholesale menu and delivery schedule.
