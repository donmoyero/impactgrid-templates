Each tomato in this trio is a heritage variety saved and shared through generations of seed-saving. The Cherokee Purple is deep, dusky and rich — the perfect sandwich tomato. Green Zebra is tangy with a firm bite, brilliant in salads. Sun Gold explodes with sweetness in every bite.

We grow them in rotation with cover crops and compost, letting the season do the work. No artificial heat, no ripening gas — just the California sun, patient soil, and a farmer who checks each row every morning.
