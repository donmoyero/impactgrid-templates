The Chef

Marco Bellini

Marco learned to cook in his grandmother's kitchen in Emilia-Romagna — not from textbooks, but from seasons. He watched her transform market finds into meals that made the whole neighbourhood gather around her table.

After 8 years in Michelin-starred kitchens across Europe — from Osteria Francescana in Modena to The Clove Club in London — Marco opened OLIVO with one idea: cook what's honest, serve it warmly, and let the ingredients speak.

"I don't design menus. I listen to what the land is offering this week, and I build the meal around that. The best dish is the one that couldn't have existed last week."

Philosophy

What we believe

Market-First Cooking

Every morning starts at the market, not the whiteboard. We buy what's beautiful today and build the menu around that — not the other way around.

Honest Ingredients

We know every farmer, every fisherman, every cheesemaker by name. Our ingredients travel miles, not continents. Seasonal isn't a trend — it's how food should taste.

Warmth Over Formality

Fine dining without the stiffness. We want you to relax, linger, and feel at home. The food is precise; the atmosphere is not.

Zero Waste

Vegetable trimmings become stocks. Bread becomes croutons. Coffee grounds become compost for our herb garden. Nothing leaves OLIVO without purpose.
