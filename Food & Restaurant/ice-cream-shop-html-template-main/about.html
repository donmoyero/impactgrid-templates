Our sweet little story

2015 Born in a cart
Since 2015

From a bicycle cart to the corner of Maple & Cherry

iCREAM began as a single hand-cranked churn strapped to a vintage bicycle cart at the summer farmers' market. Ten years, one expansion and countless happy messes later, we're a full parlour — but we still churn every batch the same slow way.

  • ✓
    Family-runThe Oliveira family still opens the shop every morning at 9am sharp.
  • ✓
    Zero shortcutsReal cream, real fruit, real honey. If we can't say where it came from, it doesn't go in the churn.
  • ✓
    Waste-nothing kitchenDay-old cake goes to the local shelter; whey feeds the neighbours' pigs.

What We Believe

Three scoops of philosophy

The rules that keep our ice cream honest — and our customers coming back.

01

Local First

If it grows or grazes within 40 miles, it's on our shortlist. Small farms, big flavour.

02

Honest Labels

Four ingredients on our vanilla label. You can read every one without a dictionary.

03

Seasonal Respect

No strawberries in January. When the orchard sings, so do we — then it's on to the next fruit.

04

Generous Hands

We scoop a little taller than we have to. It's a shop rule. Nobody has complained.
