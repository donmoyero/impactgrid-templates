Our Story

A little parlour with big dreams

iCREAM started in 2015 with a tiny churn, a vintage bicycle cart, and a stubborn belief that ice cream should taste like the moment it was made.

  • ✓
    Farm-to-cone sourcingWe know every farm, every orchard and every beehive behind our ingredients.
  • ✓
    Small-batch every dayEach flavour is churned in 40-litre batches — when it's gone, it's gone until tomorrow.
  • ✓
    Community firstSchool visits, scoop-for-a-cause nights, and a loyalty card your grandma would love.
