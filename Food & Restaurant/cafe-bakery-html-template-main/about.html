Our Story

Slow dough, local grain, and a decade of happy crumbs.

Since 2013

Baked from a Small Oven

CRUMB & CO. started as a single oven in a family kitchen, delivering bread to neighbours on a bicycle. A dozen years later we're still using the same slow-fermented recipes — only now the kitchen is a little bigger and the bicycle is a fleet of vans.

We mill our own flour where possible, source dairy from local farms, and ferment every dough for at least 24 hours. Good bread simply cannot be rushed.
