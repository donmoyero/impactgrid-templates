One spring. One aquifer.
One mountain.

RIVULET draws from a single protected spring in the Cambrian Mountains of mid-Wales — naturally filtered through 400 million years of geological history.

📍 Llanwrtyd Wells · 52.0667° N, 3.6333° W
  • 01
    400 Million Years of FiltrationRain enters the Cambrian sandstone at altitude and descends through geological layers for centuries before emerging at the spring.
  • 02
    Protected StatusThe spring sits within an Environment Agency Protected Area — no agriculture, no industry, no development within 2 km.
  • 03
    Consistent Mineral ProfileGeological stability means the mineral composition has remained virtually unchanged for millennia — consistent pH, consistent taste.
  • 04
    Bottled On-SiteOur bottling facility is 200 metres from the spring. Water is sealed within minutes of extraction — no transit, no repackaging.
