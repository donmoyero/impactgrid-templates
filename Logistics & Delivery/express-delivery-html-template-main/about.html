Our Journey

From One Van to a Global Fleet

In 2012, FASTER started in a cramped garage in Austin, Texas. Our founder, Daniel Reyes, had one conviction: that last-mile delivery could be faster, more transparent, and more human.

With a single van and a prototype tracking app, we served 12 local businesses in our first year. Word spread. By 2015, we had 50 vehicles. By 2018, we crossed the border into Mexico and Canada. Today, we operate in 50+ countries with a fleet of over 500 vehicles and partnerships with major air and sea carriers worldwide.

Our mission hasn't changed: deliver at the speed of now, with care at every step.
