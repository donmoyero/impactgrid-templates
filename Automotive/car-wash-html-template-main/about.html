About AquaShine

Our story, our team, and our commitment to excellence.

Our Story

AquaShine was born in 2017 when founder Marcus Rivera turned his garage into a detailing studio. What started as a weekend passion project quickly became a full-time operation as word spread about the quality of his work.

Today, we operate a state-of-the-art facility with 24 certified detailers, serving over 12,500 happy customers. Our philosophy remains the same: every car gets the same meticulous care, whether it's a daily driver or a weekend collector.

We invest in the latest equipment, eco-friendly products, and continuous training because we believe your car deserves nothing less than the best.
