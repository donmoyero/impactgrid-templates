Good to know

Questions we're asked most

Do you offer non-surgical options?
Yes. About a third of our work is injectables, skin and laser. We'll always advise the least invasive route that can genuinely deliver the look you want.
What does a consultation cost?
The initial private consultation is complimentary for surgical treatments. For injectables and skin, consultation fees apply but are credited against your first treatment.
How long is recovery after surgery?
It varies by procedure — from a few days of downtime for blepharoplasty to two weeks for a full facelift or abdominoplasty. You'll receive a written recovery plan with milestones before you book.
Can I finance my treatment?
Yes. We offer 0% interest plans for up to 12 months and partner with trusted financing providers for longer terms, all arranged discreetly before your procedure date.
Will my results look natural?
That is our signature. We work with restraint, plan with imaging, and — when a patient asks for more than we'd recommend — we say so honestly.
