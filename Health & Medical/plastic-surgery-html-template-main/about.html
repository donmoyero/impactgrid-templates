Our story

Born from a simple frustration with loud clinics

Plasery began in 2010, when two surgeons shared the same complaint: aesthetic medicine had become a sales floor. Bright lights, pushy coordinators, and results that shouted instead of whispered.

We built the clinic we wanted to be treated in — a place of soft light, honest answers and surgeons who take their time. Today, the same philosophy runs through every consultation, every incision and every follow-up call.

Plasery means “to please gently.” We try to live up to the name.
