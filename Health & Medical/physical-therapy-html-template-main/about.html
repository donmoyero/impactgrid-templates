Our Story

Founded by therapists who wanted more time for you

In 2012, three physiotherapists opened Terapia's doors in a small Portland studio with one rule: every patient gets a full hour and a fully personalized plan.

Fourteen years later, the rule hasn't changed. Our team has grown to ten specialists, but each visit still starts with listening — to your pain, your goals, your life. We believe the best outcomes come from a partnership: your effort, our expertise, and a shared roadmap you actually understand.

From weekend athletes to grandparents playing with their grandkids, we've helped more than 2,000 people move better, hurt less and live with more confidence. That's the work we're proudest of.

  • Licensed, continuously-trained therapists only
  • Hour-long assessments with a dedicated clinician
  • Transparent pricing and direct insurance billing

Our Philosophy

Three values behind every session

They sound simple. They're the hardest — and most important — things we do.

01 — Listen First

Understand before treating

Your history matters as much as your symptoms. We dig into how pain started, what makes it worse, and what you want your life to look like again.

02 — Teach Always

Education that empowers

We explain the "why" behind every exercise and technique, so you leave each visit knowing more about your body than you did before.

03 — Measure Progress

Evidence, not guesswork

From functional tests to movement screens, we track measurable progress and adjust your plan the moment the data says to.

How We Work

Your recovery, step by step

A clear, predictable path from your first visit to your strongest self.

1

Book & Meet

Choose a time online or by phone. Your first assessment is a full hour with a licensed therapist.
