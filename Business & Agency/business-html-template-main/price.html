FAQ

Questions about pricing

What is included in the flat fee?

Everything scoped in the engagement letter. No hidden charges, no surprise add-ons. If additional work is needed, we agree it upfront.

Can I cancel a retainer?

Yes. All retainers operate on a 30-day notice period. We believe in earning your business every month, not locking you in.

Do you offer discounts for startups?

We offer a reduced-rate Strategic Briefing for early-stage founders. Reach out and we'll find a model that works for your stage.

How are outcomes measured?

Every engagement has defined KPIs from day one. We report against them monthly and adjust our approach if targets are at risk.
