Common questions

Before you ask

How long does a visa application take?+

It depends on the country and season. Tourist visas typically process in 2–6 weeks, student visas in 3–12 weeks, and PR applications in 6–18 months. Your consultant will give you a realistic timeline after your eligibility check.

What is your success rate?+

Across all application types in the past year, 98.6% of Travisa-filed applications were approved. Where a refusal happens, we include a full re-application strategy as part of our service.

Do you guarantee approval?+

No honest consultancy can guarantee a decision — visas are approved by governments, not agencies. What we guarantee is meticulous preparation, transparent advice and our full effort at every step.

Can you help after a refusal?+

Yes. Refusal handling is one of our most valued services. We audit the reasons given, fix the weaknesses, gather fresh evidence and re-file with a stronger application.

What are your fees?+

Fees are fixed and agreed before we begin — never a percentage, never surprise charges. Government and embassy fees are paid separately and itemised clearly.
