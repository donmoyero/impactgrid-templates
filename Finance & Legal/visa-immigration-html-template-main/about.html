Our story

Started with one desk, one dream, one rejected file

Travisa began when our founder, Elena Voss, watched a qualified nurse lose a job offer because her visa paperwork had a single misfiled page.

"That wasn't her fault," Elena remembers. "It was the system's — and nobody was helping her navigate it." What started as a favour for one family became a consultancy built on a simple promise: no one should miss out on a life-changing opportunity because of paperwork.

Two decades later, that promise still guides every application we prepare — now across 24 offices, 120+ destinations, and four continents.

  • ✓ Regulated by national immigration bodies in every office country
  • ✓ 98.6% approval rate across all application types
  • ✓ Named consultant for every client — no call centres
