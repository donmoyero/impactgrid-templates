The Firm

A chambers built on candour, rigour & the quiet confidence of precedent.

Kanun was founded on a single conviction: that the finest legal advice is delivered early, plainly and without drama. What began as a two-advocate practice in a single office is today a chambers of more than forty lawyers across five practice groups.

We are retained for the matters that matter — boardroom investigations, high-value litigation, cross-border transactions — and we carry them with the discretion the trust demands. Every engagement is led by a partner, never delegated to the margins.

How We Counsel

An approach measured in candour, not complexity.

We begin every engagement the same way: by listening until the problem is fully understood, then stating plainly what the law permits, what it costs, and what we would do if it were our own matter.

  • ✓Clarity first. A written strategy and fee estimate within five working days of instruction.
  • ✓No surprises. Monthly reporting on every live matter, in plain language, from the partner leading it.
  • ✓Decisive advocacy. We pursue resolution with discipline — and when settlement serves you better than trial, we say so.
  • ✓One team. Counsel, paralegals and specialists assembled for your matter, accountable to a single partner.
