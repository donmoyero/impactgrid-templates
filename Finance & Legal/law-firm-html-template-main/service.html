How We Counsel

An approach measured in candour, not complexity.

We begin every engagement the same way: by listening until the problem is fully understood, then stating plainly what the law permits, what it costs, and what we would do if it were our own matter.

  • ✓Clarity first. A written strategy and fee estimate within five working days of instruction.
  • ✓No surprises. Monthly reporting on every live matter, in plain language, from the partner leading it.
  • ✓Decisive advocacy. We pursue resolution with discipline — and when settlement serves you better than trial, we say so.

The Kanun Standard

Four principles, kept since 1988

I

Integrity

Advice is given to serve the client's interest — even when it means advising against a costly course of action.

II

Rigour

Every pleading, opinion and agreement is reviewed by a second partner before it leaves the chambers.

III

Discretion

What is entrusted to us stays with us. Our offices, archives and counsel are bound by the same code.

IV

Advocacy

When a matter reaches the courtroom, it is prepared as though the outcome will be examined for decades.
