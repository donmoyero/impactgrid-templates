Our Story

What began as two advocates in one office is today a chambers of more than forty lawyers.

Kanun was founded on a single conviction: that the finest legal advice is delivered early, plainly and without drama. That conviction has not wavered in the thirty-seven years since.

The chambers opened its first door in a single borrowed office on Court Square in 1988, with two advocates, a shared desk and a ledger that fitted in a briefcase. The founding partner, A. Behzad Kamrani, had a simple thesis — that commercial clients were being over-served by complexity and under-served by clarity. He resolved to build a practice that told its clients the truth, early and often.

Three decades on, that thesis has carried the firm through five practice groups, eighteen jurisdictions and more than four thousand eight hundred matters. The office is larger, the ledger is digital, and the briefcase has given way to a library — but the method remains unchanged: listen until the problem is understood, then state plainly what the law permits, what it costs, and what we would do if it were our own matter.

Our Journey

Milestones of the chambers

Selected chapters from four decades of practice, marked by the matters we were trusted with and the standards we set.

1988

Founding of the chambers

Two advocates open a practice on Court Square, Manhattan, committed to partner-led representation on every matter without exception.

1995

The first practice groups

The firm formalises its work into two groups — corporate and commercial litigation — and takes its first instructions across state lines.

2003

Expansion into arbitration

Kanun establishes a dedicated dispute resolution group, and its partners begin sitting as counsel in international arbitrations.

2012

Eighteen jurisdictions

A correspondent network of independent counsel extends the chambers' reach to eighteen jurisdictions worldwide.

2019

Forty lawyers, five groups

The chambers passes forty lawyers across five practice groups, each led by a senior partner accountable for every engagement.

Today

The same standard, kept daily

Every pleading, opinion and agreement is still reviewed by a second partner before it leaves the chambers — as it has been since 1988.
