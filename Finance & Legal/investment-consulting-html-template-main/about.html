Our story

A firm built for one thing.

In 1998, our founder left a large private bank because he believed families deserved better — a single team managing their wealth with total transparency, not a product factory. Twenty-eight years later, that belief still drives every decision we make.

Today we manage over $4.2 billion for 380 families, foundations and trusts. We have no proprietary products, no sales targets, no incentive to recommend anything other than what is genuinely right for you.

Our team averages 22 years of experience. Many of us have worked together for over a decade. We are fiduciaries in law and in practice.
