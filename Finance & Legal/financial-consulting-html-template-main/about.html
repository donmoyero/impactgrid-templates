Our Story

Built on Trust, Driven by Expertise

Founded in 2003, FINANZA began with a simple mission: make expert financial guidance accessible to every business that needs it. What started as a three-person tax practice has grown into a full-service financial consulting firm serving over 2,500 clients nationwide.

  • Certified Public Accountants with deep industry specialization
  • Proactive tax strategies that have saved clients over $150M combined
  • 98% client retention rate — our reputation speaks for itself
  • Transparent pricing with no hidden fees or surprise invoices

In 2003, Elena Vasquez left a senior role at a Big Four firm with a conviction: financial consulting should be personal, transparent, and results-driven. She founded FINANZA from a single office in downtown Chicago, focusing on mid-market businesses that were underserved by both large firms and solo practitioners.

Within five years, word-of-mouth referrals doubled our client base. By 2012, we expanded into audit, advisory, and wealth management. Today, our team of 45 professionals serves clients across 12 states, maintaining the same culture of accountability that defined day one.

To empower businesses and individuals with clarity, confidence, and control over their financial futures. We combine rigorous analysis with genuine partnership — every client deserves to understand not just what we recommend, but why.

To be the most trusted financial consulting firm in every market we serve — recognized not for size, but for the depth of our expertise, the integrity of our counsel, and the measurable impact we create.
