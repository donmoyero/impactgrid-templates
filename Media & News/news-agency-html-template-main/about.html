About NEWSERS

Built for Truth.

From a single newsroom in 2012 to a global network of 14 bureaus, NEWSERS has grown on one principle: the world deserves reporting without compromise.

Our Story

A Decade of Independent Journalism

2012

NEWSERS founded in New York by a team of five veteran journalists committed to ad-free, subscriber-supported reporting. First wire dispatch published on March 14th covering the Syrian conflict.

2014

Expanded to three bureaus: London, Berlin, and Tokyo. Launched the "Unfiltered" editorial standard — no sponsored content, no native advertising, full source transparency.

2017

Broke the story on offshore financial networks that earned the agency a Pulitzer nomination. Opened bureaus in Nairobi, Singapore, and Sao Paulo.

2020

Launched the Real-Time Wire service, providing verified dispatches within 90 seconds of major events. Grew subscriber base to 2.4 million readers worldwide.

2023

Introduced AI-assisted translation across 12 languages without compromising editorial oversight. Opened bureaus in Dubai, Mumbai, and Mexico City.

2026

Operating 14 bureaus across 6 continents. Over 300 journalists, photographers, and editors. Serving 4.8 million subscribers and 1,200+ media partners globally.

Our Values

What Drives Us

☉

Independence

We accept no sponsored content, no native advertising, and no corporate influence over editorial decisions. Our revenue comes from readers and media partners — never from the subjects we cover.

☷

Accuracy

Every fact checked three times. Every source verified. We publish corrections within 15 minutes and maintain a public record of all editorial changes.

☢

Speed with Integrity

Real-time doesn't mean unverified. Our 90-second dispatch window includes mandatory fact-checking protocols developed with academic partners.

♢

Global Perspective

14 bureaus, 300+ journalists, 12 languages. We report from where the story is, not from a single viewpoint. Every dispatch carries multiple regional perspectives.

☯

Transparency

Our methodology is public. Our funding is disclosed quarterly. Our editorial standards are published in full. We believe trust is earned through openness.

★

People First

We invest in our journalists — competitive pay, mental health support, legal protection, and the resources to do meaningful work without compromise.
