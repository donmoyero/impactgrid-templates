Getting Here

Follow the Trail

Zoofari is easy to reach from the city — by car, bus or bike.

By Car

Take the A24 to Park Exit 7 and follow the green Zoofari signs. Free parking for all guests.

By Bus

The express Route 12 stops right at the main gate every 20 minutes from Central Station.

By Bike

A scenic riverside cycle path leads straight to the park — secure bike racks available.

Good to Know

Visitor FAQ

Answers to the questions guests ask most often before visiting.

Can I bring my own food and drinks?

Yes! Picnics are welcome across the park's green spaces. We also have cafés and kiosks if you'd rather eat on the go.

Are dogs allowed inside the park?

For the safety of our animals, only trained assistance dogs are permitted inside the exhibit areas. There's a shaded kennel by the main gate for small pets.

Is the park accessible for wheelchairs and strollers?

Absolutely. All main trails are paved and wheelchair-friendly, and we offer free wheelchair and stroller rentals at the welcome center.

Can I buy tickets on the day?

Yes, at the gate — but online tickets are cheaper and guarantee entry on busy days. We recommend booking ahead for weekends.

What happens in bad weather?

Zoofari is open rain or shine — many animals are more active in the cool weather! The Rainforest Loop and Nocturnal House are fully covered.

Do you offer group and school discounts?

Yes — groups of 15+ save 20%, and school visits include free educator resources. Contact our bookings team for a tailored quote.
