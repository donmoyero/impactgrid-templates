African Lion

Panthera leo

Our pride of six lions spends their days lazing on kopje rocks and patrolling the plains. Listen for the dawn chorus — it carries for miles.

African Elephant

Loxodonta africana

Our herd of seven elephants roams one of the largest zoo habitats in the region. Watch them swim, dust-bathe and communicate over long distances.

Wander the Zones

Six Worlds, One Park

Zoofari's grounds are divided into immersive habitat zones, each transporting you to a different ecosystem.

Savanna Plains

Lions, giraffes, zebras and ostriches share this open landscape with acacia-dotted horizons.

Rainforest Loop

Misty canopy walkways, tropical birds, leopards and reptiles in a dense jungle setting.

Wetland Marsh

Hippos, crocodiles, otters and waterfowl in a winding freshwater ecosystem.

Nocturnal House

Reverse day-and-night exhibits let you see bats, owls and bush babies at their most active.

Reptile Ridge

Snakes, lizards, tortoises and a walk-through butterfly aviary for the brave.

Petting Farm

Goats, sheep, rabbits and guinea pigs — the little ones' favourite corner of the park.
