Our Story

From Family Farm to a Wildlife Haven

Zoofari began in 1994 as a small family farm with a handful of rescued animals and one big dream: to build a place where people could fall in love with wildlife — and want to protect it forever.

Today that dream spans 42 hectares of rolling savanna, rainforest and wetland habitats, home to more than 120 species and hundreds of dedicated keepers, educators and conservationists.

  • 42 hectares of natural, immersive habitats
  • 120+ species cared for by expert rangers
  • Accredited by the Global Zoo & Aquarium Association
  • 60+ active conservation partnerships worldwide

Milestones

Three Decades of Wild Stories

The moments that shaped Zoofari into the park it is today.

1994 — The First Roar

Zoofari opens its gates as a small rescue farm, welcoming its first rescued lion and a handful of farmyard animals.

2003 — The Savanna Plains Open

The 8-hectare Savanna Plains habitat debuts, bringing lions, giraffes and zebras together for the first time.

2011 — Conservation Lab Launches

Our on-site conservation lab opens, partnering with field teams across Africa, Asia and South America.

2019 — Twilight Safari

The park's famous after-dark safari experience launches, drawing guests from around the world.

2026 — 400,000 Visitors & Growing

Zoofari welcomes its 400,000th annual guest and announces the next great expansion: the Rainforest Canopy Walk.
