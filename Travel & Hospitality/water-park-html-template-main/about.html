About SPLASHWAVE

A family water park born from a love of sunshine, safe water, and really, really big slides.

📖 Our Story

From a Backyard Dream to a Park-Wide Splash

SPLASHWAVE opened its gates on a hot June morning in 2014. The vision was simple: a safe, sparkling place where families could spend a whole summer day without breaking the bank. What started as a single pool and three slides has grown into twelve acres of wave pools, winding rivers, and thrill rides — but the same crew-run spirit remains.

Every morning before the gates open, our team tests every slide, checks every filter, and runs a full safety drill. Because the best day at the park is a safe day at the park.

⭐ Our Values

What We Stand For

Every decision at SPLASHWAVE is guided by four principles that keep our guests safe, happy, and coming back.

🛡️

Safety First

Daily water-quality testing, hourly slide inspections, and a lifeguard on every deck. We never compromise on your family's well-being.

🎉

Family Fun

From toddlers to grandparents, every attraction is designed so everyone can find their own wave — and a lounge chair to share.

🌿

Clean & Green

Our filtration system recycles every drop of water multiple times an hour. We use solar heating for the wave pool and compost all food waste.

🏅

Certified Crew

Every team member holds a CPR certification, annual water-safety training, and a genuine love for making people smile.
