Our Attractions

Every ride has a height requirement, a safety check, and a lifeguard ready to spot you. Here's what's waiting.

48 in +
🎢 Extreme Thrill

The Kraken Drop

60 feet of near-vertical terror.

Climb the tower, step into the capsule, and wait for the floor to drop. You'll rocket through a dark, twisting tunnel before hitting the splash pool at the bottom. Not for the faint of heart — but the adrenaline rush is unforgettable.

🛡️ Riders must be at least 48 inches tall. Maximum weight 300 lbs. Two attendants at the top of the tower at all times. Slide surface is inspected hourly.
All ages
🌴 Relax & Float

Lazy Lagoon River

1,200 feet of pure drift.

Grab a tube and let the current carry you past shaded grottos, gentle waterfalls, and a mist bridge. The river is a slow, steady loop — perfect for a mid-day reset or a sunny afternoon nap.

🛡️ Children under 48 inches must wear a lifejacket (provided free at the entrance). Lifeguard stations every 200 feet along the route. No diving.
Under 54 in
👶 Junior Zone

Splash Cove Kids Zone

Zero-depth entry for little ones.

Designed for kids under 54 inches, Splash Cove features mini slides, tipping buckets, water cannons, and a soft splash pad floor. A shaded seating area gives parents a front-row view of every squeal.

🛡️ Attendants stationed inside the zone. Maximum depth 18 inches. Swim diapers required. Free lifejackets available at the zone entrance.

More Ways to Play

Beyond the big attractions, the park is packed with extra spots to eat, rest, and explore.

🏊

Wave Pool

Six-foot swells roll through our main pool every 15 minutes. Body surfing and boogie boards welcome.

🍔

Snack Bar

Burgers, fries, ice cream, and fresh-squeezed lemonade at the Shoreline Grill. All-day dining deals available.

🏖️

Cabanas

Rent a shaded cabana with lounge chairs, a fan, and a mini fridge. Reserve yours online ahead of your visit.

🌊

Wave Machine

Our state-of-the-art wave generator produces consistent surf-quality swells. Surf lessons available on weekends.

🧒

Kids' Play Area

Dry playground adjacent to Splash Cove for a break from the water. Slides, swings, and shaded picnic tables.

Gift Shop

SPLASHWAVE hats, towels, and waterproof phone pouches. The perfect souvenir after a day in the sun.
