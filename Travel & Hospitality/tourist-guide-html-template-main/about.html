About Voyageur

The story behind the world's most curated travel guide.

Our Story

Voyageur was born on a rainy evening in Portland, Oregon, where four friends — each with a well-worn passport and a deep love for discovery — decided the world needed a better way to travel. Not the checklist kind of travel, but the kind that leaves you changed.

What started as a shared blog quickly grew into a trusted platform for curated travel. We personally visit every destination we feature, vet every local guide, and design every itinerary with intention. We believe travel should be immersive, sustainable, and accessible to anyone with a curious spirit.

Today, Voyageur connects thousands of travelers with extraordinary experiences across the globe — from the quiet coves of the Mediterranean to the misty peaks of the Himalayas.
