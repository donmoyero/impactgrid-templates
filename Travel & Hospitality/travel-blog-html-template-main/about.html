About Roamlog

The people, the values, and the journey behind the journal.

Since 2019

It Started With a Single Ticket

Three friends bought a one-way ticket to Lisbon with a single notebook and no itinerary. What began as a month of wandering became a promise: to document the world the way it deserves to be documented — slowly, honestly, and without filter.

Today ROAMLOG is a small collective of writers, photographers, and lifelong wanderers spread across four continents. We travel for weeks at a time, live with locals, and write only about places we have truly been. Every post carries the smell of a real place, because it was written there.

We believe travel is not an escape from life — it is a way of paying attention to it. Our stories are invitations: come see what we saw, and then go see it your own way.

What We Stand For

Three commitments that shape every guide, every photograph, every sentence we publish.

01

Slow Travel

We stay long enough to know a place, not just pass through it. Depth over distance, every time.

02

Local First

Guesthouses over chains, family kitchens over tourist menus, and the people of a place as our true guides.

03

Honest Words

No sponsored hype, no sugar-coated hardship. If a place disappointed us, we will tell you why.
