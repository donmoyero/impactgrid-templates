Destinations

Six places we know deeply and love honestly. Each guide is built from weeks on the ground, not a weekend of research.

Santorini, Greece

Whitewashed villages perched on volcanic cliffs, sunset views from Oia, and the bluest water you will ever see. Best visited in the shoulder seasons of May and September, when the crowds thin and the light turns honey-gold.

Caldera Views Wine Trails Sailing

Kyoto, Japan

Japan's ancient capital is a study in stillness — moss gardens, thousand-year-old temples, and geisha districts where time moves slowly. Time your visit for sakura season and arrive at the temples before sunrise.

Temples Tea Houses Sakura
Read the Guide →

Machu Picchu, Peru

The lost city of the Incas, wrapped in cloud forest at 2,430 meters. The four-day Inca Trail trek earns the view in a way no train ever can — quiet hours, mountain dawns, and stones older than empires.

Inca Trail Cloud Forest History
Read the Guide →

Tromso, Norway

Above the Arctic Circle, Tromso is the northern lights capital of the world. Ride a cable car over the fjord at dusk, feast on fresh cod at the harbor, and chase auroras on snowmobile under a sky full of stars.

Northern Lights Midnight Sun Fjords
Read the Guide →

Namib Desert, Namibia

Where the oldest desert on Earth meets the Atlantic, rust-red dunes roll into crashing surf at Sossusvlei and Sandwich Harbor. Climb Dune 45 at dawn and watch the valley paint itself in gradients of fire.

Dune Climbing Wildlife Stargazing
Read the Guide →

Ubud, Bali

Bali's cultural heart beats in Ubud — emerald rice terraces, jungle temples, and a creative community of artists and healers. Walk the Campuhan Ridge at dawn, then lose an afternoon to a warung and a good book.
