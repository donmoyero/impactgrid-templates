Our Story

Two Backpacks and a Big Idea

TripCraft started in 2011 when two friends came home from a year of travel convinced that the industry had it backwards — packaged trips felt mass-produced, and DIY planning felt overwhelming. So we built a third way: journeys designed by hand, around real people.

Today, our 60+ travel designers and 300+ local partners across five continents still craft every itinerary from scratch. We know the name of the family who runs your guesthouse, the baker who opens early for your sunrise walk, and the exact bench where the light is perfect at 5pm.

What We Stand For

Our Values

Three principles guide every itinerary we design, every partner we choose and every mile we send you on.

🌱

Travel That Gives Back

We offset the carbon of every flight and reinvest 2% of each trip into local conservation and community projects in the places we visit.

🤝

Local First, Always

Family-run stays, independent guides and neighborhood restaurants — the people who make a place worth visiting should be the ones who benefit.

🛡

Safety Without Compromise

Vetted partners, 24/7 on-trip support and flexible rebooking policies — so the only surprise on your trip is a good one.
