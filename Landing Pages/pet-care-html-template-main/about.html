Our Story

Built on Love, Care & Wagging Tails

PetLover began as a small neighborhood grooming salon with one simple dream — to give every pet the love and care they deserve. Over a decade later, we've grown into a full-service pet care center trusted by thousands of families.

From our certified groomers to our experienced veterinarians, every member of the PetLover family shares the same passion: treating your pets like our own.

Our facility features modern grooming stations, cozy boarding suites, a dedicated vet clinic, and a serene spa area — all designed with your pet's comfort in mind.
