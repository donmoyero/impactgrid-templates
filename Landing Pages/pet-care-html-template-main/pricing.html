Frequently Asked Questions

Can I customize a package?

Absolutely! We're happy to mix and match services to create the perfect plan for your pet. Just let us know what you need.

Do you offer discounts for multiple pets?

Yes! We offer a 10% discount when you book services for two or more pets from the same family.

What if I need to cancel or reschedule?

We understand plans change. Free cancellation up to 24 hours before your appointment. Late cancellations may incur a small fee.

Do you accept walk-ins?

We welcome walk-ins based on availability, but we recommend booking ahead to guarantee your preferred time slot.
