Where your gift goes

Follow the money, literally

We publish an open annual audit and geotag every single tree. Here's the split for every dollar.

🌳

66¢ · Planting

Saplings, nursery costs and the hands that put them in the ground.

🛡️

22¢ · Care & patrol

Fencing, watering, weeding and the rangers who watch over new growth.

🔬

8¢ · Science & data

Species surveys and monitoring that prove the ecosystem is recovering.

🎒

4¢ · Community

Schools, tool sheds and the volunteer teams that make it all possible.
