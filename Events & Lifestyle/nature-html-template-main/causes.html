How funds are used

Every dollar has a job

We publish an annual audit, geotag every tree and keep overhead under 12%. Here's where your gift goes.

🌳

Planting & care

Saplings, fencing, watering and three years of follow-up care so every tree has the best shot at standing on its own.

🐾

Patrols & science

Night rangers, camera-trap networks and species surveys that tell us whether the wild is actually returning.

🎒

Volunteer support

Tools, boots, transport and hot lunch for the 8,400 volunteers who turn up — and keep turning up.
