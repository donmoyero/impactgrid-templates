Good questions

Before you write in

The answers to the things we're asked most often.

🌱Can I visit one of your reserves?+
Absolutely. We host open days at Elderpine Reserve and Green Valley HQ most months. Watch the events calendar, or email us to arrange a guided visit for your school or group.
🧾Are donations tax deductible?+
Yes. Wildrealm Trust is a registered charity, and every donation over the minimum threshold is eligible for a tax receipt. You'll get one automatically by email after each gift.
🌳Can I sponsor a specific tree or area?+
You can! Larger gifts can sponsor a named grove or an entire restoration plot. Email us with "named grove" in the subject and we'll walk you through the options and the plaque.
🏫Do you run school programs?+
We do — curriculum-linked visits, nursery sessions and the Root School Gardens program that plants native gardens at local schools. Contact us to book your class in.
