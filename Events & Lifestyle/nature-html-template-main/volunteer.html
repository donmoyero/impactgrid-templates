Why volunteer

You don't have to be a scientist to save a forest.

Most of our volunteers arrive knowing nothing about trees — and leave knowing exactly what it feels like to plant one.

We provide the tools, the training, the gloves and the hot lunch. You bring sturdy shoes and a spare hour, a Saturday, or a whole season.

  • Free events all year, family friendly
  • Track the exact trees you plant
  • Certificates, references and school hours
