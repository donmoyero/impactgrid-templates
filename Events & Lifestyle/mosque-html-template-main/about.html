Our Story

A Beacon That Has Shone Since 1985

The Beacon Mosque began as a small gathering of families who dreamed of a dedicated place for their community to pray, learn, and grow together. From those humble first prayers in a rented hall, we have grown into a vibrant centre serving thousands of neighbours.

Today our doors open at dawn for Fajr and close long after Isha, filled with the sounds of children memorising the Quran, scholars teaching, volunteers serving, and neighbours simply saying hello. We remain true to our founding promise: a place where everyone is welcome.

Mission & Vision

Service, Learning, Community

Our mission is to serve Allah by serving people — offering authentic Islamic education, meaningful worship, and compassionate community service to everyone who walks through our doors.

We envision a thriving community where every person feels they belong, every seeker finds knowledge, and every neighbour finds a helping hand.
