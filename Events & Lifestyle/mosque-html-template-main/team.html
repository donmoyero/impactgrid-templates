The People Behind The Beacon

Leadership

Meet Our Imams & Committee

Our team is made up of qualified imams, dedicated scholars, and community members who lead with faith and humility.

Imam Dawud Rahman

Head Imam & Khatib

Imam Dawud has led Friday prayers and community life at The Beacon for over a decade, known for his warm, practical khutbahs.

Dr. Aisha Rahman

Resident Scholar

Dr. Aisha teaches our sisters' circle and leads the annual Seerah studies programme, bringing years of academic experience.

Shaykh Omar Hassan

Guest Scholar

A beloved visitor to The Beacon, Shaykh Omar leads weekend intensives on Quran, seerah, and contemporary ethics.

Hafiz Bilal Khan

Quran & Youth Lead

Hafiz Bilal directs our children's weekend school and youth programmes, memorising the Quran with the next generation.
