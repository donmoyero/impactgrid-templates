Activities & Programmes

Programmes for Every Age

Classes, circles, and clubs that nurture faith, build character, and bring our community together.

Weekly Programmes

Something for Everyone

From young children to grandparents, there is a class or circle waiting for you at The Beacon.

🕐 Mondays & Wednesdays · 7:00 PM

Qur'an & Tajweed Classes

Learn to read the Qur'an with correct recitation, led by certified teachers for adults of every level.

🕐 Saturdays · 10:00 AM

Children's Weekend School

Qur'an, Arabic, Islamic manners, and creative crafts — a joyful morning for children aged 5 to 12.

🕐 Tuesdays · 6:30 PM

Arabic for Beginners

Build conversational Arabic and reading confidence in a friendly, patient classroom environment.

🕐 Thursdays · 11:00 AM

Sisters' Circle

A warm space for sisters to study, share, and support one another over tea and meaningful discussion.

🕐 Sundays · 2:00 PM

Youth Sports & Leadership

Football, basketball, and mentoring for teens — building teamwork, discipline, and confidence.

🕐 Monthly & during Ramadan

Community Meals & Iftars

Shared meals that open our doors and our hearts — everyone is welcome at the table.

Plan Your Term

When Programmes Run

📅

Three Terms a Year

Classes follow the school calendar: Autumn (Sep–Dec), Spring (Jan–Mar), and Summer (Apr–Jun), with a light July break.

🕐

Evening & Weekend Slots

Most adult classes run after Isha on weeknights, while children's and youth programmes fill the weekend mornings.

🤩

Free & Open to All

No fees for most programmes. A small contribution helps cover materials, and scholarships are always available.

Get Involved

Start This Week

New members are always welcome. Simply drop by before any class, or send us a message and we will help you find the right programme.

If a class does not yet exist, we will gladly help you start it — many of our most-loved circles began with one person's idea and a cup of tea.

Register My Interest

Not Sure Where to Start?

Our team is happy to guide you to the right class or circle. Come visit, ask questions, and find your place at The Beacon.
