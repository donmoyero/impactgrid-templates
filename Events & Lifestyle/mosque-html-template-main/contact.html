Find Us

Easy to Reach, Easy to Find

The Beacon Mosque sits a five-minute walk from Riverside Park and is served by the 12 and 27 bus routes. Free on-street parking is available on Beacon Lane after 6:00 PM and all day on Sundays.

Wheelchair access is available through the main entrance, and there is a dedicated prayer space for sisters with a separate wudu area.
