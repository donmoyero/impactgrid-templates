About Us

We Don't Just Help — We Partner

Charitize was built on a simple belief: the people closest to a problem are the ones closest to its solution. Our role is to listen, fund, and amplify.

Our Mission

Rooted in Community

We believe that lasting change comes from within.

Since 2012, Charitize has worked alongside communities in 28 countries to address three fundamental needs: clean water, quality education, and economic resilience. We don't arrive with blueprints — we arrive with listening ears.

Every project begins with a community audit. Local leaders identify priorities. Our engineers and educators provide technical support and resources. And within 18 to 36 months, the community runs the program independently.

This is not charity as usual. This is investment in human capacity.

Our Values

The Principles That Guide Us

⚖

Transparency First

Every dollar tracked. Every project audited. Every impact report published openly. 92 cents of every dollar goes directly to programs.

☆

Local Leadership

We don't parachute in solutions. We invest in local leaders who understand their communities' needs, culture, and capacity for self-sufficiency.

✿

Systemic Change

We don't build wells and leave. We build the knowledge, networks, and systems so communities can maintain and replicate success independently.

Our Journey

A Decade of Impact

2012

Founded in Brooklyn

Three volunteers, one well in rural Kenya, and a conviction that charity could work differently.

2015

100 Wells Milestone

Expanded to 8 countries. Launched our education scholarship program in partnership with local schools.

2019

Community Resilience Program

Launched micro-loan and vocational training cooperatives, reaching 5,000+ families across Southeast Asia.

2023

10,000 Lives Changed

Hit the 10,000 mark. Expanded to 28 countries with a network of 4,300+ active volunteers.

2026

Looking Ahead

Goal: 20,000 lives changed by 2028. Launching a clean energy initiative and expanding mentorship programs.
