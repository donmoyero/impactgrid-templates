Our Teachers

The people holding the space

Every teacher at Sattva is certified, mentored and encouraged to teach exactly as they practice — honestly, kindly and with room for mistakes.

Maya Linden

Founder & Lead Teacher

500-hour E-RYT, Hatha, Vinyasa, Yin. Maya trained in Rishikesh and has been guiding quiet, strong practices for over a decade.

Elena Petrova

Senior Teacher — Iyengar & Yin

Elena brings precision and patience, trained in the Iyengar tradition with a focus on alignment, props and long-held rest.

Ravi Sharma

Teacher — Vinyasa & Kundalini

Ravi's classes are a journey — linking breath, movement and mantra with a warm, inclusive energy that welcomes every level.

Priya Nair

Teacher — Prenatal & Postnatal

Priya is a specialist in pregnancy and postpartum yoga, creating a safe, supported space for every stage of motherhood.

David Okafor

Teacher — Kundalini & Meditation

David weaves kriya, mantra and meditation into classes that feel both grounding and expansive. A student of the Kundalini tradition.

Millie Harper

Teacher — Hatha & Restorative

Millie's gentle voice and clear cueing make every pose feel accessible. She specialises in slow, supported practices for all ages.

Join the team

We're always learning

Every teacher at Sattva participates in a monthly mentorship circle, an annual workshop budget and a studio culture that values rest as much as asana. If you're a certified teacher and our approach resonates, we'd love to hear from you.
