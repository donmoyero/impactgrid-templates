Got questions?

Frequently asked

Can I freeze my membership?

Yes — pause your plan for up to 8 weeks per year at no charge. Just let us know 48 hours before your next billing cycle.

Is there a joining fee?

No. Every membership starts the minute you sign up — no initiation fee, no deposit, no lock-in contract.

Can I try a class before committing?

Absolutely. Your first class is free on any plan. Drop in, breathe, and see if the studio feels like home.

What if I need to cancel?

Cancel anytime from your account page. Your membership stays active until the end of the billing period.
