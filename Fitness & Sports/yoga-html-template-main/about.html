About Sattva

The story behind the studio

Sattva began with one mat, one valley, and the quiet belief that everyone deserves a place to breathe.

Our beginning

A studio built one breath at a time

In 2016, Maya Linden rented a sunlit room above the bakery on Cedar Lane, rolled out a dozen mats and offered the neighborhood free classes for a month. Forty people came. Nearly all of them stayed.

Nine years and one very loved wooden floor later, Sattva has grown into a full studio with a team of fifteen teachers, a weekend retreat lodge by the creek, and the same founding promise: nobody practices alone, and nobody is rushed.

What we stand for

Mission, held in three values

Welcome everyone

Every body is a yoga body. We teach to who is actually in the room, not to an image of a perfect pose.

Teach to the body

Props, adjustments and honest dialogue — not dogma. If it hurts, we change it. If it helps, we keep it.

Slow is a skill

We keep classes small so no one is ever lost. Our biggest ambition is that you leave slower than you arrived.
