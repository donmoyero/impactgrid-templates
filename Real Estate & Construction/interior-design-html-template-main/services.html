What We Do

Six disciplines, one team

Engage us for a single room or the entire building — every service is delivered by our in-house designers, never subcontracted.

01

Residential Design

Homes that breathe — space planning, material palettes and bespoke joinery tailored to how your household truly lives, from apartments to country houses.

02

Commercial & Workspace

Offices, studios and retail environments that express your brand while supporting focus, flow and well-being — designed around real working patterns.

03

Lighting Design

Layered light plans that shift through the day — ambient, task and accent lighting engineered for atmosphere, comfort and energy efficiency.

04

Decoration & Styling

The finishing layer — art, textiles, objects and fragrance composed so a room feels complete, personal and quietly lived-in from day one.

05

Renovation & Remodel

Sensitive transformations of existing spaces — structural re-plans, kitchens, bathrooms and extensions handled end to end with minimal disruption.

06

Turnkey Delivery

One team, one contract — we manage procurement, contractors and installation for a truly finished space you can move into the day we leave.

How We Work

A calm, four-stage process

Every project follows the same disciplined arc. You always know where you are, what happens next and what it costs.

Discover

We listen, measure and study your light, site and habits. A clear brief and a transparent budget are agreed before any pencil touches paper.

Design

Concepts become floor plans, material palettes and 3D visuals. We refine together until every corner feels exactly right.

Deliver

Detailed drawings, procurement and our project team manage every contractor and supplier to a firm schedule and budget.

Style & Handover

We style, photograph and walk you through your finished space — then hand over a full care guide so it stays beautiful.
