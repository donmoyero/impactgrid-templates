Our Story

Built on trust,
balanced with beauty.

iDESIGN was founded by Isabelle Durand in 2011, after a decade leading projects in Paris and London. Her vision was simple: a studio that treated every interior as a long-term relationship — not a transaction.

Over the years we have grown from a two-person practice into a full-service studio with nineteen permanent staff, a portfolio of 250+ projects, and a reputation for calm, rigorous delivery. We work across residential, commercial and hospitality sectors, and we never outsource the creative thread.

Our Philosophy

Three principles that guide every project

01

Listen First

We spend meaningful time understanding your habits, aesthetic and how you want to feel in a space. The design flows from what we learn.

02

Build with Depth

We layer materials, light and texture so rooms evolve through the day and age gracefully. Good design only gets better with wear.

03

Deliver with Care

We manage every detail from procurement to installation. Our project management is as thoughtful as our design work.
