What We Offer

End-to-End Construction Solutions

From initial consultation and design through construction and final handover, CONSTRA provides comprehensive services for every project type and scale.

01

Commercial Construction

We deliver commercial spaces that elevate brands and empower businesses. From office towers and retail centers to hospitality venues and healthcare facilities, our team manages every aspect including site selection, design coordination, permitting, and construction.

Includes: Site analysis, design-build, general contracting, tenant improvements, LEED certification support.

02

Residential Development

Your home deserves a builder who understands that a house is more than walls and a roof. We craft custom residences, multi-family developments, and luxury estates with meticulous attention to livability, aesthetics, and long-term value.

Includes: Custom homes, duplexes & townhomes, multi-family, pre-construction consulting, smart home integration.

03

Renovation & Remodeling

Transforming existing spaces requires a delicate balance of preservation and innovation. Our renovation experts breathe new life into buildings while respecting their original character, structural integrity, and historical significance.

Includes: Interior renovations, seismic retrofitting, historic preservation, kitchen & bath remodels, accessibility upgrades.

04

Project Management

Our certified project managers serve as your single point of accountability, coordinating architects, engineers, subcontractors, and vendors to deliver on time, on budget, and to specification. Transparent reporting keeps you informed at every milestone.

Includes: Scheduling, cost control, quality assurance, risk management, stakeholder reporting, closeout documentation.

05

Infrastructure & Public Works

Building the backbone of communities. From bridges and roadways to water treatment facilities and public parks, we deliver infrastructure projects that serve the public good with durability and fiscal responsibility.

Includes: Civil works, bridges & roadways, utilities, public facilities, environmental remediation.

06

Pre-Construction Services

Success is determined before ground is broken. Our pre-construction team provides feasibility studies, cost estimating, value engineering, and constructability reviews to set your project up for success from day one.

Includes: Feasibility analysis, budget development, schedule optimization, BIM modeling, permit coordination.
