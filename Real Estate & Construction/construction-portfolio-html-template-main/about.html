Our Mission

Building Beyond Bricks and Mortar

At CONSTRA, we believe construction is more than assembling materials. It is about creating environments where people thrive, businesses grow, and communities flourish.

Founded in 2008, we have grown from a boutique contractor into a full-service construction firm with a portfolio spanning commercial, residential, and public infrastructure projects across the Pacific Northwest and beyond.

Our approach blends time-tested craftsmanship with modern technology, ensuring every structure we deliver meets the highest standards of durability, aesthetics, and sustainability.

Our Journey

Key Milestones

2008

2012

First $10M+ Project

Delivered our first major commercial mixed-use development, establishing our reputation for large-scale excellence.
